Boardgamestats got a full visual identity in September 2025 (“Cartographer”): strategy-map colours, token-driven CSS, and a mobile-first card UI with optional dark mode. Functionally it still tracks collections, sessions, and stats for your group — the refresh is about clarity on phone and desktop alike.

Not affiliated with BoardGameGeek; BGG data is used under their API terms.
